Privacy statement

Version August 2026 · English translation of the Dutch "Privacyverklaring"

I, Roy Ketelaar (sole proprietorship, KVK 66437067), am responsible for the processing of personal data as described in this statement. It covers visitors of royketelaar.nl and clients, prospects and contact persons with whom I have business contact.

In short

  • I only process data that I need to stay in touch, do my work and send invoices.
  • royketelaar.nl uses no cookies and no trackers.
  • I do not sell data and only share it with suppliers I need, such as my hosting provider and my accounting software.
  • You can always view, correct or delete your data. Email info@royketelaar.nl.

Which data I process and why

  1. Below you find, per situation, which data I process, for what purpose, on which legal basis and for how long I keep it.
    Situation Data Purpose and legal basis Retention period
    You contact me or request a quote Name, company name, email address, telephone number, content of your message Responding to your question and preparing a quote. Legal basis: performance of the contract or the steps leading up to it. Up to 1 year after the last contact if no assignment follows
    I carry out an assignment for you Contact details, project communication, access details to accounts that you give me Carrying out the assignment and maintenance. Legal basis: performance of the contract. Up to 2 years after the end of the collaboration. Access details I delete at the end.
    I invoice Name, address, KVK number, VAT ID, bank details, invoices and quotes Administration and tax returns. Legal basis: legal obligation. 7 years (statutory retention period for tax purposes)
    You visit royketelaar.nl Technical data such as IP address, browser and time, in the server logs of my hosting provider Security and proper functioning of the website. Legal basis: legitimate interest. A short time, according to the log retention period of the hosting provider
  2. If I process data for a client, for example through a contact form on that client's website, then that client is responsible. I am then the processor. Please refer to the privacy statement of that website.

Cookies

  1. royketelaar.nl uses no cookies and no similar techniques. I do not use analytics or third-party trackers.

With whom I share data

  1. I do not sell your data. I only share it with suppliers I need for my work (processors), or if the law requires me to. I have a data processing agreement with these suppliers.
    Supplier For what Location
    Netlify, Inc. Hosting of royketelaar.nl and of websites I host for clients, DNS, server logs United States, with data centres in the EU
    Moneybird B.V. Accounting, quotes and invoices Netherlands
    TransIP B.V. Business email and domain name Netherlands
    GitHub, Inc. Storage of source code and website content in version control United States
  2. I only use suppliers outside the European Economic Area with an adequate level of protection, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the standard contractual clauses of the European Commission.

How I secure data

  1. I take appropriate measures against misuse, loss, unauthorised access and unwanted disclosure. Think of encrypted connections (HTTPS), strong passwords and two-factor authentication on my accounts, encrypted devices and limited access.
  2. Do you have the impression that your data is not properly secured, or do you see signs of misuse? Then email info@royketelaar.nl.

Your rights

  1. You have the right to access your personal data, to have it corrected or deleted. You can also object to the processing, have the processing restricted, withdraw your consent and have your data transferred.
  2. Send your request to info@royketelaar.nl. I respond within one month. To make sure the request comes from you, I may ask verification questions or contact you through the email address I know from you. I do not ask for a copy of your identity document.
  3. Are you not satisfied with how I handle your data? Then you can file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) via autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl.

Changes

  1. I may change this statement, for example if I start using a new supplier. The current version is always available on royketelaar.nl. The version date is at the top.
  2. This statement is also available in Dutch and Frisian. The Dutch text ("Privacyverklaring") is the binding version. If they differ, the Dutch text prevails.
